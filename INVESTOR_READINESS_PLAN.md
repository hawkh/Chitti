## Pitch Deck Structure (10 Slides)

### 1. Problem (1 slide)
- Manufacturing defects cost $X billion/year
- Manual inspection is slow, inconsistent, expensive
- 30% of defects missed by human inspectors

### 2. Solution (1 slide)
- AI-powered defect detection
- 95%+ accuracy in X seconds
- Reduces inspection time by 80%

### 3. Product Demo (1 slide)
- Screenshot of working system
- Key features highlighted
- QR code to live demo

### 4. Market Opportunity (1 slide)
- TAM: $X billion (NDT market)
- SAM: $Y billion (your target segment)
- SOM: $Z million (realistic 3-year capture)

### 5. Business Model (1 slide)
- SaaS: $X/month per inspector
- Enterprise: $Y/year per site
- API: $Z per 1000 inspections

### 6. Traction (1 slide)
- 3 pilot customers
- X inspections completed
- Y% accuracy improvement
- Z hours saved

### 7. Competition (1 slide)
- Manual inspection (slow, inconsistent)
- Legacy software (no AI, clunky)
- Other AI solutions (expensive, not specialized)
- Your advantage: [specific differentiator]

### 8. Go-to-Market (1 slide)
- Target: Mid-size manufacturers (100-1000 employees)
- Channel: Direct sales + partnerships
- Timeline: 10 customers by Q2, 50 by Q4

### 9. Team (1 slide)
- Founders with relevant experience
- Key advisors
- Technical capabilities

### 10. Ask (1 slide)
- Raising: $X (be specific)
- Use of funds: 50% engineering, 30% sales, 20% ops
- Milestones: [specific goals]
- Exit potential: [acquisition targets or IPO path]

## Red Flags to Avoid

### Technical Red Flags
- ❌ "Our AI is 99.9% accurate" (without proof)
- ❌ "We use blockchain/quantum/[buzzword]"
- ❌ Demo that crashes or has bugs
- ❌ Can't explain how the model works
- ❌ No discussion of edge cases or limitations

### Business Red Flags
- ❌ "We have no competition"
- ❌ "We'll get 1% of a huge market"
- ❌ No clear customer acquisition strategy
- ❌ Unrealistic financial projections
- ❌ Team with no domain expertise

### Presentation Red Flags
- ❌ 50-slide deck with tiny text

- ❌ Can't answer basic questions
- ❌ Defensive about criticism
- ❌ No clear ask or next steps

## Prepare Your Answers

### Example: Model Accuracy Question
**Bad Answer**: "Our AI is very accurate, like 95% or something"

**Good Answer**: "We tested on 500 real weld images from 3 manufacturers. Our model achieved 94.2% precision and 91.8% recall for critical defects, compared to 87% average for manual inspection. We have a detailed validation report I can share."

### Example: Competition Question
**Bad Answer**: "We have no real competition"

**Good Answer**: "Manual inspection is our main competitor - it's slow and inconsistent. There are 2 other AI solutions: Company X focuses on automotive (different domain), Company Y is enterprise-only ($100K+ contracts). We're targeting mid-market manufacturers with a $5K/month SaaS model."

### Example: Traction Question
**Bad Answer**: "We're in talks with several companies"

**Good Answer**: "We have 3 paying pilot customers: a metal fabricator processing 200 parts/day, an aerospace supplier, and a welding shop. They've collectively run 5,000+ inspections with 92% satisfaction. We have LOIs from 2 more customers pending pilot completion."
